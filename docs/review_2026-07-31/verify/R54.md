## Verdict reasoning

**What survives, verified line by line.** The gate really is pooled-only and really is the only one the
shipped layer ever saw:

```python
# scripts/bank_calibration.py:61-67
m_loio = compression_metrics(fa, ab_loio)
...
print(f"  [LOIO bound] Tier-2 top_ratio {m_loio['top_ratio']:.2f} ... "
      f"(gate top in [0.8,1.2]: {'PASS' if 0.8 <= m_loio['top_ratio'] <= 1.2 else 'FAIL'})", flush=True)
```

`compression_metrics` is pooled by construction — one ratio over the concatenated 38-image vector
(`src/calibration.py:266-271`), and `top_ratio` is a *true-mass-weighted* average of the per-image
ratios (I confirmed numerically: `Σ nᵢ·mean_predᵢ / Σ nᵢ·mean_trueᵢ = 0.857296`, identical to the pooled
value). No per-image companion is emitted anywhere in `bank_calibration.py`, while
`bank_calibration_f.py:112-136` does emit `tier2_loio_top_ratio_img_median` / `_img_p10` for the F
layer — so the asymmetry the finding describes is real. `DECISIONS.md:5049-5053` rules the pooled one is
the gate, which is (c)-deliberate for the *gate*, but the register already acknowledges that and asks
for the companion, not a re-gating. The layer is also `save()`d at line 42, *before* the gate is
computed at 60-67, so a FAIL could not have stopped the ship — that part is already filed as
`calibration-4`. Liveness is right: `models/deployable/calibration.npz` is `map_region.py`'s
`DEFAULT_CALIBRATION` (`:57`) and `src/mapping.py:272` applies `calibrate_abundance` to produce the
abundance band.

**Mis-statement 1 — the count.** 12 of 38, not 11 of 37 (above).

**Mis-statement 2 — and this is the substantive one: per-image `top_ratio` is not a level statistic.**
`top_ratio = E[pred | true > τ] / E[true | true > τ]` conditions on the *truth*, so for any predictor
with imperfect rank correlation it is biased below 1 no matter how perfectly its level is calibrated.
I made this quantitative: a predictor that is a within-image permutation of the truth — i.e. **exactly**
per-image level-calibrated, zero rank skill — scores per-image `top_ratio` of median **0.3035**, and its
floor is inside [0.8, 1.2] for only **5 of 38** images (permutation control matches the analytic floor
`mean(faᵢ)/mean(faᵢ|rich)` to 3 dp). Correspondingly the observed per-image `top_ratio` tracks per-image
**rank skill**, not level: Spearman(`top_ratio`, per-image ρ) = **+0.635**, versus +0.25 against the
floor and +0.25 against `rich_share`. So the [0.8, 1.2] band is not *attainable* per image for 33 of 38
images by any predictor short of near-perfect within-image ranking — this is the mirror image of the
review's own "Pattern A": a bar that could not be passed. The register's failure-scenario sentence
("on the typical held-out *place* the rich-tile mean is under-valued by ~1.8×, and across places the
level error spans two orders of magnitude") therefore attributes to *calibration* a deficit that is
mostly the already-documented per-image ranking ceiling (`PLAN_Calibration.md:284, 289`: per-image
ρ 0.433, "the 5 m/px CTX magnitude floor"). The level-only statistics are far milder: all-tile
`mean(pred)/mean(true)` median **1.075** (not 0.57) and self-conditioned median **0.909**.

**Why it still confirms.** The level-only statistic *also* fails the band, so the conclusion holds by a
cleaner route: LOIO per-image level ratio is inside [0.8, 1.2] for **11 of 38** images, p10 0.205 /
p90 3.43, sd(log₁₀) **0.52** — a typical per-place level error of roughly a factor of 3, with one image
(`ESP_076499_1160`, the −64° latitude outlier, mean pred 0.0009 vs mean truth 0.055) off by 100×. That
is a genuine, live, per-place defect that the pooled 0.857 does not express, and it is the exact quantity
`PLAN_RegionalMap`'s THEMIS leg will compare. The magnitude is ~3×, not ~300×.

**A correction the register got backwards — and it makes the finding sharper, not weaker.** The area file
asserts the harsher per-image statistic was measured "only for the F gate and never for the shipped one",
and `CODE_REVIEW_2026-07-31.md:229` says "the per-image companion was then never reported, so nobody saw
how far outside the band the per-image figure was". That is false: `DECISIONS.md:5525-5535` and
`reports/figures/fbuild_abort_level_vs_labels.csv` report the **shipped mosaic layer's per-observation
level ratio** (`mean(predicted abundance) / mean(labelled fractional_area)`, 95,606 tiles / 21 obs) —
median 0.89, min 0.41, max 2.10, 5.1×, sd(log₁₀) 0.170 — and conclude "*the incumbent mosaic map is
**well calibrated against truth** (median 0.89, only 5.1× spread)*". I reproduced that row exactly from
the CSV. But that row is **fully in-sample**: `models/deployable/86c51a5dca220f63/recipe.json` has
`"n_train_images": 38` with all 21 of those obs in `train_obs_ids`, and the banked calibrator is fitted on
all 38 by design (`bank_calibration.py:36-40`). The honest LOIO equivalent on the same 21 obs is median
1.19, min 0.18, max 4.75, **26.4×**, sd(log₁₀) **0.338**, **6 of 21** in band — i.e. the committed
"5.1× / 0.170 / well calibrated against truth" is optimistic by ~2× in sd(log₁₀) and is the only
per-place level number on the record for the shipped product. (The CSV's `label_mean` and `n` match
`dataset_v2/labels` exactly for all 21 obs, but `mosaic_pred` does not match
`calibrate_abundance(LOIO p_rich)`, confirming it is the deployed in-sample raster sampled at the

**This does not reopen the abort.** All five arms in that table share the same in-sample head, so the
*relative* ranking (mosaic 0.170 vs resid 0.371 vs pfree 0.532) is apples-to-apples and the abort
verdict is untouched. What is not safe is quoting the mosaic row's **absolute** 0.89/5.1× as the shipped
map's level accuracy off-HiRISE.

**Things I tried in order to kill it, that failed.** (i) *Documented?* `DECISIONS.md:5049-5053` documents
that pooled binds *the gate*, but nothing documents the per-image level spread of the shipped layer under
LOIO. (ii) *Guarded by a caller?* No — nothing branches on the gate, and the layer is saved before it.
(iii) *Pinned by a test?* `tests/` pin round-trip, monotonicity and the LOIO protocol, not the
aggregation. (iv) *Mitigated in the reader-facing docs?* Partially, and this is worth recording:
`docs/model_evidence.md:328-340` lists "THEMIS-comparable absolute abundance" as **item 3, "(needs

misleading text is internal (`PLAN_Calibration.md:311` "the LOIO numbers … are the conservative bound it
inherits" — pooled LOIO is conservative relative to *in-sample pooled*, but is the optimistic

## Corrected claim

The shipped abundance layer's Tier-2 acceptance (`top_ratio 0.8573` → PASS against the declared
`[0.8, 1.2]`) is a **pooled, true-mass-weighted** statistic, and `scripts/bank_calibration.py` emits no
per-image companion even though `scripts/bank_calibration_f.py:112-136` does for the F layer. Under the
honest LOIO protocol the **per-place level** of the calibrated abundance — `mean(pred)/mean(true)` per
image — is inside the declared band for only **11 of 38** images, with p10 0.205 / p90 3.43,
sd(log₁₀) 0.52, and one image off by 100× (`ESP_076499_1160`). The only per-place level number currently
on the record for the shipped layer (`DECISIONS.md:5534`: median 0.89, 5.1×, sd(log₁₀) 0.170, "well
calibrated against truth") is **fully in-sample** — head and calibrator both fitted on those 21 images —
and its honest LOIO counterpart is median 1.19, 26.4×, sd(log₁₀) 0.338, 6 of 21 in band.

Two corrections to the register's own version: the per-image count is **12 of 38**, not 11 of 37 (it
silently drops `ESP_047976_2020`, which has 5 rich tiles and is in band); and per-image **`top_ratio`
is the wrong instrument for this claim** — it conditions on the truth, so its floor for a perfectly
per-image-calibrated zero-skill predictor is median 0.30 with only 5 of 38 images' floors inside
[0.8, 1.2], and it correlates with per-image ρ (+0.635) far more than with anything level-like. The
per-place level error is a factor of ~3, not the ~300× the quoted 0.009-2.86 range implies.

## Fix assessment

The register's proposed fix is right in direction but should be re-pointed at the level statistic:

1. **Do** make `scripts/bank_calibration.py` emit a per-image block beside the pooled gate, copying
   `bank_calibration_f.py:112-136`. But report **`mean(pred)/mean(true)` per image** (median, p10/p90,
   sd(log₁₀), fraction in band) as the *level* companion, and keep per-image `top_ratio` only if it is
   printed **next to its own per-image floor** `mean(faᵢ)/mean(faᵢ|rich)`, or replaced by the
   self-conditioned `E[p|p>τ]/E[t|t>τ]`, which is a level statistic and not skill-limited.
2. **Do not** re-state the band [0.8, 1.2] as a per-image bar for `top_ratio`: 33 of 38 images cannot
   reach it under any calibration. Declaring it would be a fresh Pattern-A gate.
3. **Amend the record.** `PLAN_Calibration.md:311, 218, 356` should say *pooled* and drop or qualify
   "conservative bound"; `DECISIONS.md:3794-3796` likewise. Most important — and this the register
   missed — annotate `DECISIONS.md:5525-5535` / the `mosaic` row of
   `reports/figures/fbuild_abort_level_vs_labels.csv` as **in-sample** (head `n_train_images: 38`),
   note the LOIO counterpart (median 1.19, 26.4×, sd(log₁₀) 0.338), and state that this does not
   change the abort's relative ranking.
4. **The mitigation is no longer deferrable for the THEMIS leg**, but note the deferral at
   `PLAN_Calibration.md:373-376` was scoped to *OOD covariate shift*, not to within-cohort per-place
   level, so this is a new item rather than an un-deferral: before any absolute THEMIS comparison,
   either fit qmatch per region / conditioned on the predicted mean (`PLAN_Calibration.md:216-217`
   already lists this as the open refinement) or restrict the comparison to rank correlation, which
   `docs/model_evidence.md:328-334` already scopes it to.
5. Worth a separate look (outside R54): `ESP_076499_1160` at −64° latitude is a 100× level failure and
   the model predicts a mean `p_rich` of 0.021 on an image whose rich share is 0.443 — a plausible
   out-of-cohort-terrain case sitting inside the "in-distribution by construction" cohort.
