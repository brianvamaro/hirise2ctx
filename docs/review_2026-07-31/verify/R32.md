## Verdict reasoning

**The mechanism is real and correctly identified.** `src/modeling/gbm.py:419`
`kw["metric"] = ["binary_logloss", "auc"]` with `:436`
`callbacks.append(lgb.early_stopping(self.params.early_stopping_rounds, verbose=False))` (default
`first_metric_only=False`) does put valid-set AUC in the monitor, directly against
`gbm.py:382-384` ("Early-stopping metric is `binary_logloss`, not AUC …") and `PLAN_Stage5b.md:136-138`
("The classifier's early-stopping metric is `binary_logloss`, not `auc`"). `lightgbm/callback.py:414-437`
raises on the first stalling (dataset, metric) pair, so `best_iteration = min(best_iter_logloss,
best_iter_auc) + 1`; AUC is the metric that fires on 16/37 S=64 folds, sometimes drastically
(`ESP_055690_2200` 5 trees vs 399 under logloss-only; `ESP_055017_2055` 95 vs 395; `ESP_045550_2180`
4 vs 226). I could not kill this part: **(c)** `DECISIONS.md` has **zero** hits for `first_metric_only`,
`binary_logloss`, "early-stopping metric" or `best_iteration`, so it is undocumented, not deliberate;
**(d)** no test pins it — every `tests/test_modeling_gbm.py` GBM test passes `early_stopping_rounds=0`
(callback disabled via `_should_enable_early_stopping`), and the one test with `rounds=10`
(`tests/test_modeling_evaluate_classification.py:196`) asserts only aggregate key shapes.

**Two of its claims do not survive.**

1. *Causation is misattributed.* The headline ("shipping 1-tree boosters on 11 of 38 LOIO folds" as a
   consequence of early-stopping on AUC) is wrong: 9 of the 10 (S=64) and 10 of the 11 (S=32) one-tree
   folds are **binary_logloss**-driven and stay at 1 tree under the exact fix the register proposes. The
   real driver is `scale_pos_weight` (1.64–2.12 here) inflating the probabilities while the
   inner-validation set is a **single rotated image** whose positive prevalence ranges 0.0–0.99 against a
   ~0.35 training prevalence (`evaluate.py:610-628`): valid logloss is then worst-at-its-best and
   degrades from round 2, so `best_iter = 0`. Spearman(trees, inner-val prevalence) = +0.35 (S=64) /
   +0.44 (S=32). So the register's fix cannot deliver the effect the register attributes to it.
2. *Blast radius is overstated.* I re-ran the S=32 Tier-1 reference under the docstring monitor and
   re-computed both FM gates with `verdict()`'s arithmetic: pooled PR-AUC rises only 0.4840 → 0.5177 and
   the median per-image AUC — the statistic the "0.7865 median AUC" claim is compared against — actually
   *falls* 0.6631 → 0.6569. The frozen recipe's declared margins (`pooled_delta` 0.2992 vs a 0.03 bar,
   `dauc_median_v` +0.1198 with p=4.5e-8 vs a +0.05 bar) move to 0.2655 and +0.1166: **both gates still
   PASS, by 9x and 2.3x**. The FM-vs-Tier-1 verdict and the frozen recipe are not at risk.

The one number I found that does cross a line under the fix is in **closed** CNN work:
`DECISIONS.md:3110-3115`, gate (b) "fusion pooled ≥ Tier-1" was PASS with `F1(ens) 0.5160` vs Tier-1
0.4840; against 0.5177 it would FAIL by 0.0017. That entry's compound verdict was already "**recipe NOT
confirmed at S=32**" (gate (a) failed at +0.0498), so no recorded verdict flips; the affected text is
interpretive ("handcrafted features degrade badly at S=32, Tier-1 pooled 0.5651 → 0.4840" becomes
→0.5177; "the S=32 Tier-1 floor is weaker (0.484)" becomes 0.518).

Hence: real defect, real live code path (`sweep_binary.py` / `train_binary.py` / `sweep_within_image.py`
and the FM probes all instantiate this class), but measured impact bounded to ±0.03 pooled PR-AUC on a
reference floor with no verdict consequence → **medium**, not high. Two incidental notes, not re-files:
the docstring's "~0.17 % positives at S=64" is stale (the dataset_v2 base rate for `fa_gt_1e-2` is
0.354, per `binary_target.py:63-68` and the banked `base_rate`s), and `verdict()` takes Tier-1 per-image
AUC from the sweep summary (all T1 tiles) while computing the challenger's on the merged frame.

## Corrected claim

`LightGBMClassification.fit` monitors `["binary_logloss", "auc"]` with `first_metric_only=False`,
contradicting its own docstring and `PLAN_Stage5b.md §4`; valid-set AUC is the metric that actually
triggers the stop on 16/37 (S=64) and shortens 18/38 (S=32) LOIO folds of the banked Tier-1 reference,
in places by 100+ trees. However the reference's 42 %-of-folds ≤5-tree cohort and its 10–11 one-tree
folds are **not** caused by the AUC monitor — 90 % of them survive the fix, because the true cause is a
`scale_pos_weight`-inflated probability scale judged by `binary_logloss` on a single-image
inner-validation set of wildly varying prevalence. Re-running the reference under the documented monitor
raises its pooled PR-AUC by 0.034 and *lowers* its median per-image AUC by 0.006, leaving both frozen-recipe
gates passing with 9x / 2.3x headroom. The defect is a documentation-vs-code protocol violation with a
bounded, measured effect on a reference floor — not a threat to the FM-vs-Tier-1 margin.

## Fix assessment

The register's code fix is right but incomplete, and its follow-up work is now unnecessary:

- `first_metric_only=True` or dropping `"auc"` from `kw["metric"]` does align the code with
  `gbm.py:382-384` and `PLAN_Stage5b.md:136-138` — do one of them (and keep `auc` reportable via
  `valid_names`/`record_evaluation`, not the monitor).
- "then re-run the Tier-1 reference and re-state the FM-vs-Tier-1 margin wherever it is quoted" —
  I have already run this at S=32; the numbers are above. Nothing needs re-stating except two
  interpretive figures in the closed CNN section (`DECISIONS.md:3110-3140`, ~3435) and the gate-(b)
  sign there. Re-banking the reference is optional; a DECISIONS note recording the measured
  counterfactual is cheaper and sufficient.
- What the fix does **not** do is remove the truncated folds. If short fits are the real worry, the
  levers are: a multi-image inner-validation set (or pooled inner-val) instead of one rotated image;
  monitoring `average_precision`/`auc` *deliberately* (and rewriting the docstring to say so — note the
  measured median per-image AUC is slightly *better* with AUC in the monitor); computing
  `scale_pos_weight` only for the objective while monitoring an unweighted, prevalence-robust metric;
  and `min_delta > 0`. Independently, log/assert `booster.best_iteration` per fold so a 1-tree fold is
  visible instead of silent — that part of the register's fix is unambiguously worth doing.
