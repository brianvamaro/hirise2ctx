## Verdict reasoning

**Case (f) on the code, case (e) on the blast radius.**

*The sentinel collision is exactly as described and is reachable.* `src/striping.py:251` is
`out = np.clip((a - med) / iqr * s0 + m0, 0, 255)` and `:252` is `out[arr == 0] = 0` — the floor is
`0`, and `0` is unambiguously the nodata sentinel here: `src/striping.py:231` ("valid (DN>0; mosaic
nodata=0)"), `src/striping.py:82` (`ctx[ctx == 0] = np.nan`), `src/mapping.py:78`+`:95`
(`(box == 0).mean()`), `src/mapping.py:256-257` (`usable = valid & (zero_frac <= max_zero_fraction)`).
The synthetic run reproduces the whole chain: a valid DN-40 pixel is written as `0` and a 32×32 box
of them reports zero-fraction 1.00.

Refutations tried and failed:
- **(a) factually wrong?** No. The clip really is `[0, 255]`; `out[arr == 0] = 0` is a no-op for
  pixels the clip already floored, so it does not rescue them.
- **(b) unreachable / guarded?** No. There is **no separate nodata mask**: `predict_window` infers
  nodata solely from `arr == 0` on the array it is handed, and `scripts/striping_a1_map.py:132` and
  `scripts/striping_a1_infer_crop.py:91` hand it the *A1-normalised* array (both re-inline the same
  `0, 255` clip at `:93` / `:88`). Directly measured: 13 / 38 cohort images have `thr ≥ 1 DN` and
  11 / 38 actually produce zeroed valid pixels on the training path.
- **(c) deliberate/documented?** No. `git log -S` shows the line was born in `830a39b` (the A1
  prototype) and never touched; no `DECISIONS.md` / `PLAN_StripingArtifact.md` entry mentions the
  floor; and **three sibling implementations of the same stretch clip to `[1, 255]` with the
  rationale written down** — `scripts/f_region_stageb.py:76-77` (docstring `:60`: "uint8 (nodata/<=0
  → 0)"), `scripts/f_pilot_crop.py:165` (the *same* `A1_M0=125 / A1_S0=27.7` formula), and
  `scripts/f_leg_b_embed.py:76`. The register's Stage-B comparison checks out.
- **(d) pinned by a test?** No. `tests/test_striping.py:19` asserts `out.min() >= 0`, which a
  `[1,255]` clip also satisfies; `:26-30` is a constant array; `:33-40`'s `np.diff(...) >= 0` is
  satisfied by a flat clipped run. The proposed fix breaks no test.

**What does not survive is the blast radius — the part the register leans on.**

*Liveness is wrong.* No shipped artifact goes through `a1_apply`. `reports/map_a1/` **does not
exist** (that is R06), and the post-abort deliverable `reports/map_region/` is built by
`scripts/map_region.py`, which imports only `src.mapping` and never touches `src.striping`. The
shipped raster therefore cannot carry the defect. What *has* run through the clip is
`dataset_v2/fang_embeddings_a1` → `models/deployable_a1` → the two banked A1 numbers, plus any
future A1 map. The register's own "fix before building any A1 map" is the accurate framing; its
`live-shipped` tag is not.

*The pixel damage is ~0.04–0.4 %, not "the boulder shadows".* Measured on the actual native pixels:
the **training** path (which produced the banked −0.024) zeroes **0.041 %** of valid pixels — it
cannot have moved a pooled AUC by 0.024. The **deploy** path (160 m statistics) zeroes **0.41 %** on
the same imagery. My deploy simulation is an upper bound for the region: it derives the 160 m IQR
over an ~8 km HiRISE window (median 22.0 DN) whereas real deployment derives it over a whole ~28 km
source frame (cohort median 27.7 DN = `A1_REF_IQR`), which lowers `thr`. So the true region-wide
deploy figure is bracketed **0.04 % – 0.41 %** of native pixels.

*The differential-footprint η² confound did not occur.* numerics-1's most consequential downstream
claim is that the published "≈28 % η² reduction" mixes coverage with mitigation because
`striping_a1_infer_crop.py:98-101` builds `fb`/`fa` from each arm's own finite mask. For that crop
the masks were identical: `DECISIONS.md:4133` records "218,089 of 219,961 160-m prediction tiles",
and with `R0,C0,SIZE = 1504, 8992, 15008` (`:40`) the grid is 469² = 219,961 with an interior of
467² = **218,089** — i.e. every non-edge tile came back finite, so **zero** tiles were dropped by
`max_zero_fraction` in either arm. The 28 % is a like-for-like comparison.

*What genuinely survives on impact.* The floor does fire on real data, and it is **concentrated in
low-IQR frames** — precisely where a robust *gain* is largest. On the deploy statistic, individual
tiles reach `own_tile_zero_fraction = 1.00` (whole tiles blacked out, e.g. in `ESP_046328_2180`,
`ESP_049242_2115`, `ESP_054622_2240`), **6.7 %** of tiles carry at least one clip-blackened pixel
while still passing the mask (so they are silently embedded with false-black pixels), and **0.375 %**
would be dropped as "data gaps". The worst frame in the committed 380-frame table
(`K02_054159_2164_XI_36N007W`, 160 m IQR 6.4) has `thr = +138.7 DN`. And R07's aggravation is real
and now quantified: the 160 m statistic clips **10×** more native pixels than the native one.

Net: a real, undocumented sentinel collision that fires on real data, biased toward exactly the dark
terrain the model keys on, but at a level far too small to have changed anything measured or
shipped. Downgraded from `high` to `medium` (wrong results in a narrow, identifiable scenario — the
low-IQR frames of a future A1 map), not refuted.

## Corrected claim

`a1_apply` (`src/striping.py:251`) and its two inline copies (`scripts/striping_a1_map.py:93`,
`scripts/striping_a1_infer_crop.py:88`) clip to `[0, 255]`, colliding with the Murray mosaic's nodata
sentinel: a valid pixel with `x ≲ med − 4.51·iqr` is written as `0`, is thereafter counted as a data
gap by `own_tile_zero_fraction` (`src/mapping.py:95`) and excluded by `a1_stats`
(`src/striping.py:231`), and its texture is collapsed onto the floor. Nothing documents the choice,
and three sibling implementations of the same stretch use `[1, 255]` and say why.

**Measured blast radius: 0.041 % of native pixels on the training path and 0.04–0.41 % at deploy;
0.64 % / 6.7 % of tiles carry a false-black pixel; 0.04 % / 0.375 % of tiles would be dropped as
nodata; concentrated in low-IQR frames where whole tiles go black.** No shipped raster is affected
(`reports/map_a1/` does not exist; `reports/map_region/` never calls A1). The banked −0.024 LOIO cost
and the banked 28 % η² reduction are **both unaffected** — in particular the alleged
differential-footprint confound in the η² comparison did not occur (218,089 = 467² = the complete
interior grid in both arms). Fix it before any A1 map is built; it invalidates nothing on the record.

## Fix assessment

The register's fix — `np.clip(..., 1, 255)` in `src/striping.py:251` plus the two script copies, with
the scripts calling `a1_apply` instead of re-inlining it — is **correct and sufficient for the
sentinel collision** and matches the convention already used three times elsewhere. Cheap, low-risk,
test-safe (no assertion in `tests/test_striping.py` pins the floor). Do it before `scripts/striping_a1_map.py`
is ever run. Four qualifications:

1. **`[1, 255]` fixes the *sentinel* problem, not the *information* problem.** The dark tail still
   collapses, now onto DN 1 — which is itself the Murray bottom-clip value, a pathology this repo has
   already been bitten by (`DECISIONS.md:2725-2732`: two images bottom-clipped to DN=1 made DN 1 the
   modal DN and silently killed all four shadow features until `_compute_dn_thresholds` was taught to
   exclude `DN <= 1`; see also `:2868`, `:2902`). The clean fix is to carry an explicit boolean
   nodata mask alongside the array — e.g. have `striping_a1_map.a1_window` return the raw
   `window.data == 0` mask and have `predict_window` take a `nodata_mask=` argument instead of
   inferring it from `arr == 0` — or to widen `s0` / soft-clip. `[1,255]` is the right stopgap.
2. **Log the clipped fraction per frame** (numerics-1 already says this). It is the cheap guard that
   would have made this measurable without the analysis above, and it is what surfaces the low-IQR
   frames where the clip actually bites.
3. **Drop the "re-score the A1 payoff η² on a common finite mask" sub-fix** — unnecessary work. The
   masks were already identical (218,089 = 467² in both arms).
4. **Changing the floor changes the training input.** `dataset_v2/fang_embeddings_a1` and
   `models/deployable_a1` were baked with floor 0. At 0.041 % of training pixels a re-bake is not
   required for correctness, but train and deploy should be changed together (re-embedding the cohort
   is ~14 min per `DECISIONS.md:4120`); the far larger train/deploy mismatch on this path is R07's
   160 m-vs-native statistic, which is the one that actually matters.

The sibling sub-finding that `a1_stats` substitutes `iqr = 1.0` on a zero IQR (`src/striping.py:236`,
`... or 1.0`) is real (confirmed by reading; `frame_stats_160` guards `iqr > 0` at
`striping_a1_map.py:78`, `a1_normalize_window` does not) but effectively unreachable — it needs a
constant window, and no image in the 38-image cohort has a zero native IQR (min measured 17 DN).
Returning `nan` is a safe tidy-up, not why R38 matters.
