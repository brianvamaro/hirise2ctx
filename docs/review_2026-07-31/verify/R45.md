## Verdict reasoning

**(f) — real and correctly stated in substance.** Everything I tried to kill it with failed:

- **(a) is the LOIO arm also scored per quadrant?** No. `notebooks/_build_10.py:799-802` aggregates the
  within sweep with `groupby('held_out_obs_id').agg(within_auc=(auc_col,'mean'))` over rows that
  `src/dataset.py:240-242` defines as one quadrant per fold (`test_mask = (q_arr == quadrant_idx) & keep`);
  `_build_10.py:803-808` takes the LOIO arm as `loio_baseline[...][['held_out_obs_id','auc']]`, one row
  per image, whose `presence_auc` was computed over the whole held-out image
  (`src/modeling/evaluate.py:343`). Confirmed empirically: 152 within folds whose `n_tiles` partition
  each image, 38 LOIO folds covering whole images. Identical structure in the probe
  (`_diag_within_image_deltas.py:50-66`).
- **(c) deliberate and documented?** No — and the near-miss is worth recording so nobody re-files it as
  "known". `PLAN_Stage5c.md:233-235` *does* notice the population difference, but analyses it as a
  **variance** problem only: *"Single-fold-level metrics on a within-image quadrant test set are noisier
  than LOIO (fewer tiles per test fold — 25 % of an image vs all of an image). The 4-fold aggregation per
  image recovers most of that."* — and `:226-228` then declares the per-image aggregate *"the headline
  number directly comparable to LOIO."* That analysis is exactly right about noise (my random-partition
  null puts the size effect at 0.000 mean) and exactly wrong about bias. No document anywhere addresses
  commensurability; `DECISIONS.md:1124` and `:1321-1322` restate the null unqualified.
- **(d) pinned by a test?** No. `tests/test_within_image_split.py` pins the partition (disjoint, covering,
  cross-scale coherent, 3 train group codes, `buffer_tiles` behaviour) — nothing about how the two arms
  are compared.
- **(b) unreachable?** No: the numbers are published in two reader-facing documents.

**Does the mismatch bias in the claimed direction, and by how much?** Yes, and I can now separate the
two candidate mechanisms, which the register conflates. Scoring the **LOIO model's own predictions** on
the **same quadrant tile sets** (exact fold membership from the within run's `predictions.parquet`,
joined on `obs_id, ti, tj`) costs it **−0.020 / −0.022 / −0.018 / −0.014** AUC at S=8/16/32/64. A
random-partition null holding the four group sizes fixed but shuffling membership costs it
**0.000**. So the handicap is **entirely spatial blocking**, not sample size: a quadrant cannot be
scored on the between-quadrant abundance contrast (mean within-image spread of quadrant base rates
0.245 at S=8), and the whole-image AUC is. The handicap is the same order as the published deltas
(+0.008…+0.028), so it is not a rounding-level nuisance.

**Direction: it manufactures the null.** The bias depresses the within arm relative to LOIO, pushing Δ
toward zero — the dangerous direction when the published conclusion *is* a null. Matching the
populations more than doubles the deltas and turns `two_stage` S=8 and S=16 significant
(p = 0.0015, 0.0003), with S=32's bootstrap CI [+0.0022, +0.0744] also excluding zero. On the metric
CLAUDE.md actually mandates (`meaningful_auc` at fa > 1e-2 — presence AUC is banned by the project's own
reporting standard) the null fails at **all four** scales matched (p ≤ 0.0011) and at two scales even
*unmatched*. So `docs/modeling_results.md:980-982` ("every CI brackets zero and no Wilcoxon p < 0.05")
and `:763-765` ("statistically indistinguishable") are not supportable.

**What survives, and why I did not escalate to blocker.** The pre-declared decision rule at
`_build_10.py:704-706` is *"Within-image AUC >> LOIO AUC (≥ 0.7) → per-image generalisation is the
binding constraint."* Matched or not, the within-image AUC is 0.567–0.677. The H5 *interpretation*
("~0.6 is a per-tile 5 m/px signal limit; more HiRISE images will not unlock it") therefore stands on
the rule that was declared in advance. What dies is the statistical claim the documents actually print,
and the "three independent measurements converge" framing built on it (`DECISIONS.md:1124-1128`).

## Fix assessment

The register's fix is half right.

- **"Score the LOIO arm on the same quadrants"** — correct, necessary, and sufficient to fix the bias.
  Implement it in `_diag_within_image_deltas.py` (the live path), restricting the LOIO run's
  `predictions.parquet` to each within-fold's **own tile set** (join on `obs_id, ti, tj` against the
  within run's `predictions.parquet`), **not** to quadrants re-derived from
  `dataset_v2/splits/within_image_4fold.json` — the JSON is a different vintage (3.5 % of tiles
  disagree). Report the whole-image LOIO AUC beside it, as the register says.
- **"or bootstrap the quadrant-size effect and report it as the null"** — **invalid**. I ran that null:
  it is 0.000 at every scale. Reporting it would certify the mismatched comparison as unbiased and
  entrench the error. Delete this branch.
- **"Re-run `within_image_4fold` with `buffer_tiles ≥ 1` before quoting the matched Δ as an effect
  size"** — nice-to-have, not blocking. Excluding cut-line bands of up to 16 tiles from *both* arms
  moves the matched Δ by ≤ 0.003 (S=8) / ≤ 0.004 (S=16) while dropping 12 % / 24 % of the tiles, so
  cut-line adjacency is not what is producing it. Cheap enough to keep as a confirmation, but the
  matched Δ should not be discounted as an upper bound on that basis.

Two additions the register does not make:

- **Switch the metric.** Redoing §9.4 on `presence_auc` violates CLAUDE.md's reporting standard
  (`feedback_no_presence_auc`). It is already banked per fold as `meaningful_auc` (fa > 1e-2), and on
  that metric the matched Δ is +0.036…+0.076 at p ≤ 0.0011 across all four scales — i.e. the standard
  the project already adopted makes the correction *more* consequential, not less.
- **Fix the sentence independently of the re-pairing.** "Every CI brackets zero" is false of the table
  as banked: `classification` S=16 is `[+0.000455, +0.0542]`, displayed as "+0.000". And restate §9.4 /
  §9.5 / `docs/modeling.md:474-480,602-612` / `PROMOTION_QUEUE.md:222-229` against the **pre-declared
  ≥ 0.7 rule** (`_build_10.py:704-706`), which H5 passes cleanly, rather than against a significance
  test it does not.
