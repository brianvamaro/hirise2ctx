## Verdict reasoning

**The mechanism is real, and I confirmed it more strongly than the register did.**

`scripts/f_h4_level.py:94-105` assembles exactly the weighted graph Laplacian `L` and `AᵀWb`. For any
connected component *c*: `1_cᵀL = 0` (rows within *c* sum to zero; no coupling leaves *c*), and
`1_cᵀAᵀWb = Σ_e w_e δ̄_e (1_c[i] − 1_c[j]) = 0` because every edge has both endpoints inside *c*.
Hence `λ·1_cᵀo = 0`, so `mean(o_c) = 0` exactly for λ > 0; at λ = 0 the min-norm `lstsq` puts
`o ⊥ null(L) = span{1_c}`, same result. My toy graph reproduces this to 3e-15 at every λ, and shows
the mean over *any union of whole components* is also exactly zero — while the same graph made
connected gives group means spread over 4.7 logits. So the between-component level really is
identically zero, not estimated.

The decisive empirical check is the one the register did not run: **I predicted which observations
must receive the gauge constant purely from graph topology, before looking at any offset value, and
got the banked set exactly right** (17 of 28, set equality). For those 17 observations
`obs_off = −median(o_raw)` is forced for *any* input data whatsoever. That is not "measured to be a
constant" — it is *incapable* of being anything else. The reason is structural and worse than "21
components": `f_h4_legb.build_edges:74-77` only ever pairs frames **within the same observation**, so
the graph carries no cross-obs constraint at all except through the 7 CTX frames that happen to serve
two HiRISE observations. `PLAN_H4_Leveling.md:48-57` pre-declared pooled PR-AUC / prec@5% precisely
because they "DO see cross-frame level changes" — and the correction handed to them has no
cross-frame level component to see for 17 of 28 observations.

**But three parts of the claim do not survive, which is why this is CONFIRMED-BUT-MIS-STATED.**

1. **"Could not have failed" is false.** The gate is monotone and well-behaved in the applied
   differential. Holding the offset field's *shape* fixed and scaling it: ×0.5 → −0.0044, ×1 →
   −0.0104, ×1.5 → −0.0180 (still PASS), ×2 → **−0.0274 (FAIL)**. The observed −0.0104 already burns
   **52 % of the −0.02 tolerance**. Under the counterfactual the register itself invokes — a real
   between-obs level of the build's magnitude — the same instrument fails 88 % (sd 1.0) to 100 %
   (sd ≥ 1.46) of random draws. The instrument was not broken; it was handed a treatment ~5× too
   small. "A gate that could not fail" (§3 Pattern A) is the wrong label for it.
2. **"The between-obs level is zero by construction" is right for 17 of 28 obs, not all 28.** The 11
   partial-component observations (coupled through those 7 shared frames) receive genuine,
   overlap-derived relative levels spanning −0.962 … +0.730, and they are exactly where the −0.0104
   comes from — the register's own self-refutation (b) establishes this and then does not carry it
   into the headline.
3. **"A measurement of nothing" overstates it.** It is a measurement of a real but doubly attenuated
   treatment: the solved frame offsets had rms 0.653 logits (within-obs; max 1.553 —
   `f_h4_legb_perframe_obs.csv`), the obs-mean step reduced that to sd 0.308, and the
   between-component part was structurally zero. What was scored is neither the frame-level
   correction nor a cross-image level.

**Not (c) "deliberate and documented", but close to it on the *fact*.** The fragmentation caveat is
carried in all four documents the finding names — `DECISIONS.md:4541-4543`, `PLAN_StripingArtifact.md`
leg-B row, `PLAN_H4_Leveling.md` §3.1 DONE block, `ROADMAP.md:19` — and in `notebooks/_build_28.py:611`.
What is understated is the *strength*: every one of them says "mostly within-obs" / "the cross-obs
skill effect is **modest** by construction", where the correct statement is "for 17 of 28 observations
the cross-obs component is **identically zero**, forced by the normal equations irrespective of the
data". The notebook goes furthest wrong: "leg-B now confirms the skill is genuinely preserved, **not
just preserved-by-metric-choice**" (`_build_28.py:642`) is the one sentence this finding directly
refutes. Not (d) either — no test pins the leg-B gate; `tests/test_leveling.py:167-179` pins
*per-component gauging* in the successor `src/leveling.py`, i.e. the codebase later accepted the
premise the leg-B script did not.

**Two corrections to blast radius, one in each direction.**

*Aggravating (missed by the register):* the reopening scorecard Brian actually saw
(`DECISIONS.md:4750-4752`) lists **two** skill numbers — "pooled PR Δ −0.0104 / deploy Δ −0.0007".
The second is `scripts/f_h4_legb_perframe.py`, built in response to the 2026-07-15 review flagging the
obs-level approximation. But it re-uses `legb.build_edges` + `h4.solve_offsets` on the *same*
21-component graph (`:97-99`), so it inherits the identical blindness — and its own output confirms
it (`approx_err = pr_auc(lev) − pr_auc(legb) = 0.0000`). The scorecard's two independent skill
instruments are **one instrument counted twice**.

*Mitigating:* "authorised ~265 CPU-h + 33 GPU-h" over-attributes. The reopening rule was
`η² ≲ 0.05 at skill ≥ −0.02`; the η² half (pilot 0.128→0.0505) and the non-circular held-out edge-CV
(0.074→0.035) came from the 7-frame pilot and are untouched by this defect, as is THEMIS ρ
0.068→0.137. The leg-B PASS was necessary-but-not-sufficient, the call was explicitly
"reopen-**with-guards**" after a YELLOW adversarial review, and the 265 CPU-h figure is itself a
probe-extrapolated midpoint. Nothing shipped depends on it: the abort went the other way and A1 /
mosaic is the deliverable. Under the protocol's calibration this is "a real protocol defect with
bounded impact" → **medium**, not high. It is not refuted, and it should not be re-filed: the
mechanism is proved.

One incidental correction for the record: `DECISIONS.md:4542` and `notebooks/_build_28.py:611` both
say "only 11 frames span >1 obs". The true count is **7** (the 11 is the number of *observations*
that take a strict subset of a component — a different quantity that happens to be 28 − 17).

## Corrected claim

> **The H4 leg-B skill gate scored a treatment ~5× weaker than the one it was supposed to test, and
> for 17 of 28 observations the treatment was mathematically forced to be one identical constant.**
> `(L + λI)o = AᵀWb` pins `mean(o_c) = 0` exactly for every connected component at every λ (min-norm
> `lstsq` gives the same at λ = 0), and `f_h4_legb.build_edges` only ever creates *within-observation*
> edges — so on the 58-frame / 47-edge / **21-component** leg-B graph the between-observation level,
> the only thing a pooled cross-image metric responds to, is zero by construction except through the
> 7 CTX frames shared by two HiRISE observations. I confirmed this by predicting the exact set of 17
> observations that receive `−0.0753` from graph topology alone. On top of that, the obs-mean step
> discards half the remaining amplitude (frame-level rms 0.653 → obs-level sd 0.308).
>
> The gate is **not** incapable of failing: it is monotone in the applied differential and fails at
> ×2 the observed spread (−0.0274), and would fail 88–100 % of the time against a genuine between-obs
> level of the build's magnitude (sd ≥ 1.0). The correct reading of the banked −0.0104 is therefore
> *"a within-obs, 5×-attenuated null result that still consumed 52 % of its tolerance"* — not
> *"leveling preserves skill"*, and specifically not the notebook's "not just
> preserved-by-metric-choice". When the same statistic was computed on real one-component offsets at
> build scale it read −0.0295 (`resid`) / −0.0891 (`full`) / −0.1856 (`pfree`).
>
> Aggravating: the second skill number on the reopening scorecard (deploy Δ −0.0007,
> `f_h4_legb_perframe.py`) re-solves on the same 21-component graph and is blind in the same way, so
> the scorecard's two skill instruments are one. Mitigating: the η² and edge-CV halves of the
> reopening bar are independent of this defect, the call was "reopen-with-guards", and the
> 265 CPU-h / 33 GPU-h ledger row is an estimate, not a measured spend.

## Fix assessment

The register's fix is correct in substance but should be sharpened, and part of it already exists in
the codebase:

1. **"Assert `n_components == 1` before scoring a cross-image instrument"** — right, and the pattern
   is *already implemented* for a sibling statistic: `src/leveling.heldout_edge_cv`
   (`src/leveling.py:439-440`) skips held-out edges whose endpoints fall in different components and
   reports the skip count, "never a number computed across two components (that would silently invent
   a comparison)", pinned by `tests/test_leveling.py:193-200`. The fix is to **generalise that
   existing guard to the skill instruments**, not to invent it. `src/leveling.regauge(comp=)` and
   `patch_graph_holes`' `component_gauged` provenance are the same lesson, learned later.
2. **"Report the sd/IQR of the offsets actually applied beside the metric delta"** — right, and
   should be strengthened: report it *at the level the shift is applied* (obs-mean, sd 0.308), beside
   the level it was *solved* at (frame, rms 0.653), beside the comparator the number will be used to
   authorise (build scale, sd 1.46–6.45). Any one of those three alone hides the attenuation.
3. **Add:** state the *coverage* of the instrument — how many scored units received a shift that was
   data-dependent (here 11 of 28 offset-bearing, 9 of 36 scored with |Δ| ≥ 0.05) versus forced by the
   gauge (17 of 28).
4. **Retraction wording.** Do *not* record "the gate could not have failed" — that is refutable (×2
   fails) and a future session will re-open it. Record: "the leg-B row is a within-obs null result at
   ~5× attenuated amplitude; it is not evidence that build-scale cross-image leveling preserves
   skill." Amend `DECISIONS.md:4534-4551`, `PLAN_StripingArtifact.md` leg-B row, `PLAN_H4_Leveling.md`
   §3.1 DONE block, `ROADMAP.md:19`, and `notebooks/_build_28.py:642`; and note that the
   `deploy Δ −0.0007` companion on the `DECISIONS.md:4752` scorecard is not an independent instrument.
5. **Incidental:** correct "only 11 frames span >1 obs" → **7** in `DECISIONS.md:4542` and
   `notebooks/_build_28.py:611`.

Interacts with **R41** (every tolerance in the programme is ±0.02 with no sampling spread): the
cluster bootstrap in `stats-fallacies-3` puts the leg-B delta at [−0.0272, +0.0018], so even the
attenuated measurement does not establish its own PASS.
