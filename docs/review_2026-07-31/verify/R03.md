## Verdict reasoning

**Clause A of the title — "HiRISE pixel scale is an unaccounted label confound" — survives, and is
substantially *understated*.** This is category (f), reinforced by a direct measurement the register
did not make.

`src/labeling.py:108-111` applies one global metre floor. I measured what that actually does to the
shipped Stage-1 detections. The two cohorts do not overlap at all: the smallest surviving polygon is
0.83–1.37 m² in every 0.25 m/px image and 2.97–4.45 m² in every 0.50 m/px image. The global
`min_size_m: 1.4105` (= 1.5625 m²) sits *below* the coarse cohort's natural floor, so it removes
**exactly zero** of the 0.50 m/px cohort's 3.1 M polygons — it is structurally incapable of
equalising the two regimes, which is precisely what `DECISIONS.md:891` says it was chosen to do
("leaves the 0.50 m/px images untouched"). The consequence is bigger than R03 implies: **on average
67 % of a fine image's labelled boulder *area* (39.8–89.3 %) lies below the coarse cohort's detection
floor**, against 2.6 % for the coarse images. I confirmed that area is genuinely in the target —
`dataset_v2/labels/{ObsId}.parquet` `boulder_area` recovers 93–99 % of the polygon-area sum, so the
5×-sub-pixel rasteriser is effectively area-preserving and the small boulders are in
`fractional_area`. So the primary regression target has a per-image minimum boulder size that varies
~3× across the cohort and is recorded **nowhere** — not in the manifest reader path, not in
`{ObsId}.json`, not in `DATA_DICTIONARY.md`.

Not category (c): the *asymmetry* is documented (`DECISIONS.md:891`, `:1355-1362`) and the deferral
was an explicit Brian decision, and R03 says so. What is undocumented is the **magnitude**, and R03 is
right that the deferral's stated trigger (`ESP_056165_2200`) went moot without the 2:1 mix going away.

**Clause B — "and it is a material part of the mosaic's level floor", at 15.8 % — does not hold as
stated.** Every digit reproduces, but the statistic will not carry the weight put on it:

1. **It is an uncorrected 1-df R² on n = 20 with a 6/14 split.** Adjusted R² = 0.111; the expected R²
   for an *arbitrary* binary covariate at this n is 1/19 = 5.3 %, its permutation median is 2.5 % and
   its 95th percentile is **19.9 %** — i.e. a coin-flip covariate beats 15.8 % about 8 % of the time.
   The bootstrap 95 % CI is **[0.3 %, 47 %]**.
2. **It is one-image fragile.** Leave-one-out moves it over 0.078–0.223; dropping
   `ESP_045983_2270` alone halves it.
3. **Pixel scale is aliased with observation footprint in this subsample.** Spearman(`MapPixel_mpp`,
   n tiles) = +0.643, p = 0.002. Adding `log10(n)` takes the scale coefficient from +0.1485 (p 0.082)
   to +0.1220 (**p 0.264**); with label level and latitude too, +0.088 (p 0.445).
4. **It does not replicate at larger n on the shipped product.** The same per-image level ratio
   computed from `striping_a1_loio_preds.csv` (the shipped A1 head, LOIO, 36 usable images, 12/24)
   gives a variance share of **0.2 %** with the gap in the **opposite direction** (+0.05 dex,
   Spearman p 0.79). `fbuild_calibration_f_loio.csv` `top_ratio` (n = 34): 0.25 %, perm p 0.82.

So "≈16 % of the incumbent's 0.170 floor is label noise" is not supported; R03's own status line
concedes p = 0.083 is "suggestive, not established", but the **title**, the §1 headline
(`CODE_REVIEW:154`), the verify scope table and the "Why it matters" paragraph all propagate the bare
15.8 % as if it were established. That gap is the mis-statement.

**What I found instead is a better version of the same claim.** Using the *measured* per-image size
floor rather than the manifest column: with the two blanks resolved (both 0.5 m/px, so
`ESP_017355_2260` is **not** an unknown third fine image — it is the coarsest observation in the set,
min polygon 5.57 m²), the n = 21 binary share rises to 17.1 % (perm p 0.062). Better, the
mechanistically correct *continuous* covariate — each image's share of labelled boulder area below
6.25 m² — gives **R² 0.238, Spearman −0.614, permutation p 0.0040** on the mosaic arm, and survives
controlling for label level (β p 0.042) though not for footprint (β p 0.111).

**Two honest caveats on that stronger version, which is why I did not upgrade the severity:**
- The covariate still correlates with the level error *within* the coarse cohort (ρ −0.446, n = 15,
  over an afb range of only 0–0.03), so what it tracks may be "small-boulder-dominated terrain that
  5 m/px CTX cannot see" rather than pixel scale per se. Pixel scale is a partial determinant of that
  size distribution, not the whole of it — the two are perfectly aliased between cohorts and cannot be
  separated at n = 21.
- The sign is inconsistent across arms: ρ(afb, log ratio) is −0.614 for the mosaic but **+0.497
  (p 0.022)** for `h1only` and +0.430 for `pfree`. A pure *label* effect would move every arm the same
  way, since the denominator is shared. Decomposing, afb tracks the labels (ρ −0.539, β −0.261) *and*
  the mosaic predictions (ρ −0.609, β −0.527) — the mosaic simply tracks it harder. So part of this
  is a prediction-side effect, not label noise. R03's "important negative" (h1only 0.2 %) is
  statistic-dependent, but its *conclusion* survives: accounting for the covariate would make the F
  arms look worse, not better, so the abort is untouched either way.

**Liveness — corrected in scope, not in tag.** R03 tags this `live-shipped`, which is right, but for
the wrong object. `fbuild_abort_level_vs_labels.csv` is a **dead-closed** artifact of the aborted F
programme; only its mosaic row (0.170) is live-cited (`DECISIONS.md:5534`, `ROADMAP.md:18`), and the
pixel-scale share of that row is what fails to establish. The genuinely live object is the **label
basis itself**: `dataset_v2/labels/*.parquet` is the training target of the frozen head behind the
shipped A1 map, so the deployed abundance field is calibrated against a target whose minimum boulder
size varies ~3× by image and is unrecorded. That matters for `PLAN_RegionalMap`'s remaining thermal /
Rodriguez-2016 legs (ACTIVE), which compare abundance *between places* against external products that
have their own size conventions.

**Severity medium, not high.** The mechanism is real, live and larger than stated, but (i) the
quantitative claim that would have made it `high` — that it invalidates part of the incumbent's
calibration floor — does not survive; (ii) the underlying asymmetry was a documented, explicit
deferral; (iii) nothing computes a wrong number today. It is a real protocol/provenance defect with a
now-quantified, bounded cost.

## Fix assessment

R03's three fix items are all correct in direction; two need amending and one should be added.

- **(a) recompute the level table with pixel scale as a covariate.** Do it, but with **(i)** the
  measured per-image floor / area-share-below-6.25 m² rather than the manifest column, **(ii)** a CI
  or permutation p reported alongside, and **(iii)** `log10(n_tiles)` in the model. Report it as
  *not significant after adjustment* — at n = 21 it cannot be decided. Do **not** publish
  "16 % of the 0.170 floor is label noise".
- **(b) fill the two blanks.** Cheaper than stated and already actionable offline: both `.LBL`s are in
  `cache_v2/pds_labels/` and both read `MAP_SCALE = 0.5 <METERS/PIXEL>`. Note the fix's premise is
  slightly off — `src/pds_labels.py` fetches and parses labels but exposes no `MAP_SCALE` accessor;
  the parse already exists at `src/colour.py:116` and
  `scripts/probes/_boulder_size_audit.py:35-43`. Better still, source the manifest column from the
  `.LBL` rather than the spreadsheet so a non-spreadsheet ObsId can never come through blank again.
- **(c) decide per-image `min_size_m` vs accept.** My measurement changes the cost side of that
  decision materially: enforcing the coarse 6.25 m² floor globally would delete ~67 % of the fine
  images' labelled boulder area, i.e. it is a re-definition of the target and a full Stage-4-onward
  rebuild, not a tidy-up. Recommend **accept + record**, not re-filter.
- **(d) — missing from R03, and the item I would actually do.** Emit the per-image effective floor as
  provenance: add `map_scale_mpp` and the measured `min_polygon_area_m2` to the Stage-1 sidecar
  `cache_v2/reprojected_detections/{ObsId}.json` and to `{ObsId}.json` in `dataset_v2/labels/`, and
  add a row to `dataset/DATA_DICTIONARY.md` under `fractional_area` stating that the target's minimum
  boulder size is per-image and cohort-dependent (0.83–1.37 m² vs 2.97–4.45 m²). That is what makes
  the accepted heterogeneity auditable, and it is what `PLAN_RegionalMap`'s cross-place validation
  legs need in order to state what the deployed abundance number's size floor actually is.
