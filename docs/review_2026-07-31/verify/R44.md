- vClaire v2 (39 solved / 38 swept): `:596-649` — §5.4's heading is literally
  "Results (vClaire 40-image dataset)", §5.6 says "dropped from the **v2** modeling dataset;
  38 of 39 images proceed".

Those two blocks contradict each other inside one document: `:294` "The **current manifest** reduces
to **nine** images carried through every stage" vs `:646-647` "38 of 39 images proceed" through
Stage 4/4b/5. `:120` "Ten HiRISE Observation IDs make up **the current dataset**" and `:1159` "at
the **current** 9 images" assert a currency that has been false since 2026-05-28. And the banner

contains content committed six days *later* at `479688d`, so it is not a snapshot of `b9bc82a` at all.
So this is **(f) real and correctly stated** on the half-migration itself. Refutation (c) fails:
`grep -n 'methods\.md' DECISIONS.md` returns only the 2026-05-22/23/28 update entries — nothing
declares the v1 sections frozen — and `docs/index.md:23-25`'s three planned documents do not include
a v2 pipeline Methods doc.

**But the headline is mis-stated on the part that matters most.** The area file's title says the
document's "target-distribution statistics are **wrong** by 2×–70×". They are not wrong. Every v1
number in §§2/6/7/8 reproduces *exactly* against `dataset/` — tile counts to the unit (643,910),

13 Spearman ρ to 3 dp, both CV schemes' fold membership and row counts. And the sections name their
cohort: `:777` "Across the **nine retained manifest images**", `:791` "in the **priority10** sweep".
The 2×–70× is the **v1↔v2 gap**, not an error. The correct charge is *stale scope*, not *wrong
arithmetic* — which matters, because it changes the fix from "recompute" to "relabel".

**Why medium, not high.** Blast radius, judged independently of the register's audience argument:

tier. Liveness `live-shipped` stands: I verified both routes verbatim.

## Corrected claim

was migrated to the vClaire v2 cohort at `479688d` (2026-05-28); §§1–4 and §§6–9 still describe the

dataset", `:294` "The current manifest reduces to nine images carried through every stage",
`:1159` "at the current 9 images") — the second of which is directly contradicted by `:646-647`
("38 of 39 images proceed"). The banner (`:3-4`) pins the document to `b9bc82a`, which is dated
2026-05-22 (not the stated 2026-05-25) and predates content the file contains. The v1 statistics
themselves are **not wrong** — all reproduce exactly against `dataset/` — they describe a superseded
cohort whose target distribution differs from the shipped one by ~2× in zero fraction, 21× at P99 and
70× in mean `fractional_area`. Separately, four numbers are wrong against the current caches
regardless of cohort: `:247` (13,352 attributed to 10 images; the 10 contain 14,292), `:369`
(four SP1-corrected; six sidecars carry the correction), `:607-613`/`:627` (the `dy` column and the
"every dy is negative" prose predate the 2026-06-10 y-sign fix; the cache now has all 39 `dy`
positive), and `docs/index.md:14` ("5 quantitative tables"; there are 7). Finally `:1035-1037`'s
causal claim is retracted project-wide (`DECISIONS.md:3530-3536`) and explains at most a 4× of the
weakness even on v1's own data (tie-structure Spearman ceiling = 0.249 vs observed max 0.083).
